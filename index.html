Meet the maker

Hi, I'm Kevin. Fox & Fir Woodworks began as a hobby in my garage in Lawrenceville, Georgia and grew into a small business because I couldn't stop building.

It's a one-man shop, and my wife Rachel pitches in when she can. Every planter and tray is designed, cut, and assembled by hand, with attention to the details you'll see every day.
